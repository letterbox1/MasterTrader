# Goal

Build **MasterTrader**: a custom, self-hostable AI trading system. It combines the best parts of leading open-source trading projects into one platform, built on free and open-source data pipelines and free-tier APIs as far as possible.

## The platform must

- **Ingest** market, macro, fundamental, news, social and global-event data from free/open sources. Global events include natural disasters, wars and geopolitical issues.
- **Research**: generate, analyse, backtest, simulate, stress-test and refine trading strategies.
- **Decide** with AI agents (multi-agent debate, valuation tools, RAG memory with reflection on past decisions).
- **Detect and explain** anomalies across prices, volumes, sentiment and events.
- **Execute** automatically through broker and exchange connectors, behind deterministic risk controls. Execution moves from paper trading to live.

## Asset and market scope

- **Trading:** crypto, forex, commodities, precious metals, stocks, ETFs, bonds, IPOs.
- **Tracked:** ICOs / token launches, crypto airdrops, social engagement signals, forex and index reports and forecasts, and global events.

## Non-goals for v1

- Guaranteed profitability claims.
- Unattended live trading without human-approved mandates.
- Scraping in breach of a platform's terms of service.
- Copying GPL/AGPL code into the core.
